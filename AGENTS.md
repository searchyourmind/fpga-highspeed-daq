- Implement and test; do not substitute plans or teaching exercises for work.
- Read PROGRESS and NEXT_SESSION before continuing. Preserve original starter provenance.
- English artifacts, concise Chinese progress messages.
- RTL: active-low synchronous reset, rising-edge ready/valid transfers; keep stalled payload stable.

- Record exact commands, test counts, tool versions, failures/fixes, and unresolved limitations.
